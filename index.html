Welcome to Prime Physiotherapy

Experienced care, close to home.

Prime Physiotherapy has proudly served the Liverpool and South Western Sydney community for more than 30 years, providing experienced physiotherapy care close to home.

Our postgraduate-qualified physiotherapists combine advanced skills, extensive clinical experience and ongoing professional education to provide high-quality treatment across our practices.

With modern facilities, onsite parking and APA Titled Musculoskeletal Physiotherapists, we are committed to providing the level of physiotherapy care our local community deserves.
